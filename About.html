FOUNDER

"As an engineering graduate, my journey through modern education made one reality very clear: memorizing textbooks doesn’t prepare a young mind for the real world. At the same time, seeing hardworking, low- and middle-income families struggle under exorbitant school fees convinced me that something had to change. Sathrunya School is my dream project—born out of a commitment to bridge this gap. I founded this school to serve our community, not to run a business. Our focus is to take learning off the printed page and bring it into the real world through curiosity, hands-on exploration, and practical skills. Every child, regardless of family background, deserves access to high-standard education that teaches them how to think, build, and solve real-life problems."

- Saikumar Duddeda,Founder

OUR VISION AND MOTIVE

My vision for establishing Sathrunya School comes directly from reflecting on my own school journey and realizing everything I was held back from achieving because the system was strictly confined to memorizing textbooks, endless notebook writing, and mechanical exam preparation without genuine understanding. During my student years, I constantly felt a deep void where curiosity, practical creativity, and real-world application should have been nurtured, leaving so many natural talents undiscovered simply because they were never tested beyond printed pages. I saw how conventional classrooms conditioned young minds to fear making mistakes instead of encouraging them to dismantle problems, observe mechanisms, and learn by doing.

This personal realization transformed into my life's core mission: I want to build a truly transformative future for children right from their early foundational stages by breaking away from the outdated routine of passive reading and writing, replacing it with deep, real-time practical exposure where every child has the freedom to touch, experiment, investigate, and explore how the world truly functions. My deepest motive is to ensure that whatever dreams, modern skills, technical confidence, and practical capabilities my generation missed out on or could not achieve during school life will be achieved and mastered by this rising generation. Sathrunya School is built on the unwavering belief that high-quality, practical education must never be treated as an expensive business or a luxury reserved for the privileged few; it is a sacred service dedicated to lifting up children from poor, lower-income, and middle-class backgrounds who carry immense potential but lack access to affordable modern platforms. By keeping our costs minimal, removing financial anxiety from hardworking parents, and transforming the campus into an active laboratory of hands-on discovery, my aim is to cultivate independent thinkers, problem solvers, and leaders who will step into tomorrow with real practical wisdom rather than just another school certificate.
